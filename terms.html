Subscriptions

call-ur-mom Premium is an auto-renewable subscription, offered monthly or yearly. Premium lets you keep up with more people (up to 10, compared with 3 during the free trial). The price of each plan is shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, and may vary by country.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Your subscription renews automatically for the same length of time unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends. If the price changes, Apple tells you in advance as its rules require.
  • You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renew at any time after purchase in your Apple Account settings, as described below.

Free trial

If the app offers you a free trial, it is shown before you subscribe, along with its length and the price you will pay when it ends. During the trial you can use the app at no charge with up to 3 people. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your subscription starts automatically and your Apple Account is charged for the first period. Apple decides who is eligible for a free trial, and a trial is generally available once per Apple Account. If you buy a subscription during a free trial, any unused part of the trial ends at that time.

Cancelling

To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose call-ur-mom, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also do this in the App Store app: tap your account picture, then Subscriptions. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Deleting the app or your call-ur-mom account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it with Apple as described above.

Invites

The app may let you share an invite code with friends. When a friend uses your code and pays for Premium, you each get a free month. These terms apply to invites:

  • Who can use a code. Your friend needs to be signed in, and neither their call-ur-mom account nor their Apple Account can have had Premium before. If they are in their first free trial, they can still use a code before it ends. Each account can use one code, any time before its first payment, and can't change it later. You can't use your own code, or use a code from another account to invite yourself.
  • When a friend counts. A friend counts once their first payment for Premium goes through. That's the first renewal at full price after a free trial, or a purchase paid on the first day. A free trial, a free month, or a purchase waiting for a parent's approval doesn't count.
  • Your friend's free month. Once their first payment goes through, your friend gets 1 free month of Premium.
  • Your free months. You earn 1 free month for each friend who counts, up to 5 free months each calendar year. The year is decided by the date each friend's first payment goes through, in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). Friends who count after your 5th free month that year still show in your invites, but they don't earn a free month, and they don't carry over to the next year.
  • Earned free months never expire. They're kept until you redeem them in the app.
  • Redeeming. Free months are App Store promotional offers, which you accept in the app through Apple's purchase sheet. They start at your next renewal (on a yearly plan, your next yearly renewal), and your subscription then renews at its usual price unless you cancel. Apple offers them only to people who have subscribed, so if you haven't subscribed yet, your free months are kept until you do.
  • Refunds. If the payment that made a friend count is refunded before you redeem the free month it earned, that month is taken back. Once you've redeemed it, it stays yours. In the same way, if your friend's first payment is refunded before they accept their own free month, that month is taken back.
  • No cash value. Free months can't be exchanged for money, sold, or moved to another account.
  • Fair use. Share your code only with people who might want the app, and don't send it as spam or post it in App Store reviews. We may refuse or take back free months earned through fake accounts, inviting yourself, or other abuse, and close accounts used this way.
  • Changes. We may change invites or end them at any time. If we do, free months you've already earned stay yours to redeem.

Reminders and Screen Time blocking

The app uses your notifications and Apple's Screen Time framework, with your permission, to remind you to call and to limit access to apps until you do. You can turn off the app's notifications or Screen Time access at any time in the iOS Settings app. Reminders and blocks depend on iOS delivering notifications and running Screen Time schedules, so they can occasionally be late or not appear. The app is a personal habit tool: do not rely on it for anything urgent or safety-related.
