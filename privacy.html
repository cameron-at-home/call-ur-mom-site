Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the call-ur-mom iOS app ("the app") and our launch email list collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Both are operated by Call Ur Mom LLC ("we", "us").

The short version: the people you choose to call, your reminders, and your call history stay on your device. We use your account details to sign you in, and basic analytics and crash reports that are not tied to your account to understand how the app is used and fix problems. Apple handles all payments and tells us your subscription status, which we keep with your account. If you use invite codes, we also keep a record of your code, who invited you, and the free months you earn. If you ask to hear when the app launches, we use your email address to send you that one message, then delete it. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We never receive it, and it is not sent to Firebase or any other server we use, apart from the fixed categories and counts described under App analytics.

  • Contacts you select. With your permission, the app reads the names and phone numbers in your contacts to show you a list to choose from. It saves only the contacts you add: their name, one phone number, and the identifier iOS uses for that contact, along with the reminder schedule you set.
  • Reminders and connection history. Your reminder schedule, the calls, FaceTime calls and in-person visits you log, the call length you choose, and any short note you add. Your stats and streaks are calculated on your device from this history.
  • Notifications. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and can include the name of the person you're due to call.
  • Screen Time. With your permission, the app uses Apple's Screen Time framework to limit access to apps until you make your call. If you choose specific apps, Apple gives the app only opaque tokens for them, so we cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them. Your selection never leaves your device.
  • Call detection. When you start a call from the app, it remembers on your device which contact you called and when, for up to six hours, so it can ask whether you reached them. It also uses Apple's CallKit to notice whether an outgoing call connected. iOS does not tell the app who you called or give it your call history, and this signal is not stored.

Signing out does not erase this information. Deleting your account in the app, or deleting the app, removes it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this information is included in those backups, which Apple's terms govern and which we cannot access.

Information we collect

  • Account information. You need an account to use the app. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores a user ID for your account, the sign-in method you use, and when you created the account and last signed in. For security, it also keeps the IP address and device information used to sign in.
    • Email and password: your email address and a securely hashed password. Firebase sends password reset emails to this address when you ask for one.
    • Sign in with Apple: your email address (or the private relay address Apple creates if you choose to hide your email) and an identifier from Apple. Apple may offer to share your name, but the app does not save it.
    • Sign in with Google: your name, email address, a link to your Google profile photo, and an identifier from Google.
  • App analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find problems. It automatically collects an app instance identifier (an ID for this install of the app, not your advertising identifier), your device model, iOS version, app version, language, approximate location (such as country or city) derived from your IP address, and usage events such as app opens, sessions, screens viewed, app updates, and in-app purchases (the product, price and currency). The app also records events about how its features are used: finishing an onboarding step; granting or declining a permission; adding a contact, including your first, and the reminder frequency chosen; completing a reminder, how it was completed (a call the app detected, a call you logged, or a visit) and the stage it had reached; skipping a reminder and the stage it had reached; a block starting, being snoozed (with the number of snoozes left) or ending (with the reason); pausing a contact's reminders (with the number of days); sharing your invite code; seeing the subscription screen (with what led to it); and starting a trial or buying a plan (with which plan). These events contain only fixed categories and counts. We do not send Google Analytics your account ID, email address, the names or phone numbers of your contacts, who you called, your notes, or which apps you limit with Screen Time, and we do not link analytics data to your account. The app does not ask for permission to track you, so iOS does not give the app or Google your device's advertising identifier.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google the next time you open the app. A report contains what the app was doing when it crashed (a technical stack trace), your device model, iOS version, app version, device state such as free memory and storage, and an identifier Crashlytics creates for this installation of the app. We do not send it your account ID, email address, contacts, call history, notes, or Screen Time data, and we do not link crash reports to your account.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. The app receives your subscription status from Apple on your device, such as which plan you have, whether it is active, and when it renews or expires. When you subscribe, the app adds an identifier made from your account ID to the purchase, so we can match Apple's records of it to your account. The app sends us the details of your purchase, and Apple's servers send us an update when your subscription renews, changes or ends. Both are signed by Apple. From them we keep one subscription record for your account: your plan, when it renews or expires, its status (such as active, in a billing grace period, expired or refunded), whether it was a real or test purchase, when Apple signed the details, and whether they came from the app or from Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • Invite codes. The app may let you share an invite code with friends, so you can each earn free months of Premium as described in our Terms of Use. For this we keep:
    • Your invite code and when it was made.
    • If you enter a friend's code: the code, which account it belongs to, when you entered it, and where your invite stands (joined, counted once your first payment goes through, or refunded), with the date it counted. If you enter the code during a free trial, we also keep the App Store transaction ID of that trial, to check that the code isn't being used from the inviter's own Apple Account. We record when you accept your own free month.
    • For each free month you earn: when you earned it, which friend's invite earned it, the App Store transaction ID of that friend's first payment, and when you redeemed it or when it was taken back after a refund.
    We don't keep names, email addresses or contacts for invites. The person who invited you sees only counts, such as how many friends joined and subscribed, never who. When you share your code, you choose the app and the people to send it to, and we don't learn who they are. If you enter a code and turn out not to be eligible, we keep nothing about it, and delete your invite record if we had already saved one.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.
  • Launch email list. If you ask to hear when the app launches, you give us your email address through a Google Form, which also records when you submitted it. You don't need a Google account to sign up.

How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account secure
  • To provide reminders, Screen Time blocking, and the app's other features
  • To provide the subscription you bought and keep a record of its status
  • To run invite codes: check that a code can be used, count the friends who join and subscribe, and give the free months earned
  • To understand app usage, fix problems, and improve the app
  • To respond to you when you contact us
  • To send the launch email you asked for, which announces the app and any launch offer

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Service providers

We share information only with the companies that run parts of the app and the launch email list for us, and only to provide them:

  • Google provides Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions for Firebase and Cloud Firestore (which hold subscription and invite records), Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Sign-In, our email, and the form and spreadsheet that hold the launch email list. Google processes this information on our behalf: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, notifications and Screen Time. Apple's privacy policy applies to the information you give Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • An email service may send the launch email for us. If we use one, it receives your email address only to send that message.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Retention

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Subscription and invite records are kept for as long as your account exists, then deleted as described under "Deleting your account".
  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Information on your device stays until you delete it, the contact it belongs to, your account, or the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need them to help you and keep a record of your request.
  • The launch email list is deleted within 30 days after we send the launch email, or if we decide not to launch the app. We remove your address sooner if you ask.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Settings, tap Manage Account, then tap Delete Account. This deletes your Firebase account, your subscription record and your invite records, and erases the app's information on your device. You can also email us from the address on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Some invite records involve two people. If friends used your invite code, their invite records stay theirs but no longer point to your account. If you used a friend's code and it earned them a free month, that month stays theirs: we keep its record, including the App Store transaction ID of your first payment, but remove the link to your account.

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it with Apple first: open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

Your rights and choices

  • You can turn off the app's access to your contacts, notifications or Screen Time at any time in the iOS Settings app. Some features stop working without them.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the account, subscription and invite information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We will ask you to write from the email address on your account so we can confirm the request is yours.
  • You can leave the launch email list at any time by emailing support@call-ur-mom.com from the address you signed up with, or by using the unsubscribe option in the launch email.
  • We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom residents. We rely on your consent to hold your address on the launch email list and send you the launch email. You can withdraw your consent at any time as described above, which does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it. You also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your address, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as that law defines those terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. In the past 12 months we collected the categories described above: identifiers (such as your email address, account ID, invite code and app instance identifier), commercial information (subscription status, purchase events, and invite and free month records), internet or other electronic network activity (app usage events), and approximate location. We collected them from you, from your device, and from Apple or Google when you sign in with them, for the purposes listed in "How we use information," and disclosed them only to the service providers listed above. You or an authorized agent can make a request by emailing us.

Children

The app and the launch email list are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If a family uses Apple's Ask to Buy, Apple asks a parent or guardian to approve a purchase. Apple handles that request, and we receive no information about the family. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and where information is processed

Information sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit, and information on your device is protected by iOS. Subscription and invite records are stored in the United States. Google processes account, analytics and launch email list information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
