Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the call-ur-mom iOS app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is operated by Call Ur Mom LLC ("we", "us").

The short version: the people you choose to call, your reminders, and your call history stay on your device. We use your account details to sign you in, and basic analytics that are not tied to your account to understand how the app is used. Apple handles all payments. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. We never receive it, and it is not sent to Firebase or any other server we use.

  • Contacts you select. With your permission, the app reads the names and phone numbers in your contacts to show you a list to choose from. It saves only the contacts you add: their name, one phone number, and the identifier iOS uses for that contact, along with the reminder schedule you set.
  • Reminders and connection history. Your reminder schedule, the calls, FaceTime calls and in-person visits you log, the call length you choose, and any short note you add. Your stats and streaks are calculated on your device from this history.
  • Notifications. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and can include the name of the person you're due to call.
  • Screen Time. With your permission, the app uses Apple's Screen Time framework to limit access to apps until you make your call. If you choose specific apps, Apple gives the app only opaque tokens for them, so we cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them. Your selection never leaves your device.
  • Call detection. When you start a call from the app, it remembers on your device which contact you called and when, for up to six hours, so it can ask whether you reached them. It also uses Apple's CallKit to notice whether an outgoing call connected. iOS does not tell the app who you called or give it your call history, and this signal is not stored.

Signing out does not erase this information. Deleting your account in the app, or deleting the app, removes it. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, this information is included in those backups, which Apple's terms govern and which we cannot access.

Information we collect

  • Account information. You need an account to use the app. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores a user ID for your account, the sign-in method you use, and when you created the account and last signed in. For security, it also keeps the IP address and device information used to sign in.
    • Email and password: your email address and a securely hashed password. Firebase sends password reset emails to this address when you ask for one.
    • Sign in with Apple: your email address (or the private relay address Apple creates if you choose to hide your email) and an identifier from Apple. Apple may offer to share your name, but the app does not save it.
    • Sign in with Google: your name, email address, a link to your Google profile photo, and an identifier from Google.
  • App analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to find problems. It automatically collects an app instance identifier, your device model, iOS version, app version, language, approximate location (such as country or city) derived from your IP address, and usage events such as app opens, sessions, screens viewed, app updates, and in-app purchases (the product, price and currency). We do not send it your account ID, email address, contacts, call history, notes, or Screen Time data, and we do not link analytics data to your account. The app does not ask for permission to track you, so iOS does not give the app or Google your device's advertising identifier.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. The app receives your subscription status from Apple on your device, such as which plan you have, whether it is active, and when it renews or expires. We never receive your payment details.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How we use information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account secure
  • To provide reminders, Screen Time blocking, and the app's other features
  • To provide the subscription you bought
  • To understand app usage, fix problems, and improve the app
  • To respond to you when you contact us

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Service providers

We share information only with the companies that run parts of the app for us, and only to provide the app:

  • Google provides Firebase Authentication, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Sign-In, and our email. Google processes this information on our behalf: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, notifications and Screen Time. Apple's privacy policy applies to the information you give Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Retention

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Analytics data is kept for [CONFIRM: 2 months or 14 months, matching Google Analytics > Admin > Data retention for the call-ur-mom property], then deleted automatically.
  • Information on your device stays until you delete it, the contact it belongs to, your account, or the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as we need them to help you and keep a record of your request.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Settings, tap Manage Account, then tap Delete Account. This deletes your Firebase account and erases the app's information on your device. You can also email us from the address on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it with Apple first: open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

Your rights and choices

  • You can turn off the app's access to your contacts, notifications or Screen Time at any time in the iOS Settings app. Some features stop working without them.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the account information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We will ask you to write from the email address on your account so we can confirm the request is yours.
  • We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as that law defines those terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. In the past 12 months we collected the categories described above: identifiers (such as your email address, account ID and app instance identifier), commercial information (subscription status and purchase events), internet or other electronic network activity (app usage events), and approximate location. We collected them from you, from your device, and from Apple or Google when you sign in with them, for the purposes listed in "How we use information," and disclosed them only to the service providers listed above. You or an authorized agent can make a request by emailing us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If a family uses Apple's Ask to Buy, Apple asks a parent or guardian to approve a purchase. Apple handles that request, and we receive no information about the family. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and where information is processed

Information sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit, and information on your device is protected by iOS. Google processes account and analytics information in the United States and other countries where it operates.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
